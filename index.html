About

Hi, I'm Sdooodly; an artist working primarily in acrylic, oil, and watercolour on canvas. I'm drawn to colour, texture, and the quiet moments between brushstrokes. c:

Painting is how I make sense of things. Each piece starts as a feeling and slowly finds its shape on canvas. I love the unpredictability of oil, the boldness of acrylic, and the softness of watercolour.

I take workshops for anyone curious about picking up a brush. Feel free to reach out, I'd love to connect.

Classes & Workshops

I offer private and mini group sessions in acrylic painting. Whether you're a complete beginner or looking to refine your technique, I'd love to work with you.

I also run summer painting classes for kids, a fun way for young ones to explore colour and creativity on canvas.
